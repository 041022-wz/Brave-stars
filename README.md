# ⭐ Brave-Stars

> Special Education · Open Resources · AI Empowerment


## Overview

**Brave-Stars** is an online platform dedicated to **special education resource sharing** and **AI-powered support**. It addresses three critical challenges:

- **Uneven distribution of resources** – Teachers in underdeveloped areas lack access to quality training.
- **Delayed diagnosis** – Most schools rely on subjective experience rather than data-driven tools.
- **Lack of family support** – Parents of children with special needs receive little professional guidance.

### Core Modules

1. **Special Education Course Library** – Curated video courses and materials
2. **Community** – A space for teachers, parents, and researchers to connect
3. **AI Chat (prototype)** – Affective AI for emotional support and practice


## My Motivation

Growing up, I witnessed firsthand how early intervention can transform a child's life — and how devastating it is when families lack access to timely support. My mother's work in special education gave me a window into both the challenges and the breakthroughs.

This project is my first step toward using technology to address these inequalities.


## My Role

This is a **solo project**. I handled everything from concept to implementation:

- Needs analysis and product definition
- UI/UX design and front-end development
- Documentation and portfolio preparation

During the process, I faced challenges with LLM deployment and consulted a professor, who recommended starting with **Llama**. This led directly to my first internship at **Hanyi Fonts** (AI LLM department), where I gained hands-on experience in multilingual data pipelines.
