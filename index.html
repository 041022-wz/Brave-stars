AI-Powered Emotional Support
Affective AI for Stress Relief and Communication

Our AI chat system is designed to recognize and respond to human emotions, providing a safe space for users to practice conversations and release pressure.

Key Capabilities
  • Emotion recognition from text input
  • Empathetic and supportive responses
  • Conversation practice for social skills
Target Users
  • Children with communication challenges
  • Parents needing emotional support
  • Teachers managing classroom stress
Technical Foundation
  • NLP & Affective Computing: Sentiment analysis, emotion classification, and empathetic response generation
  • LLM Integration (Planned): Llama 3 / Qwen for personalized, context-aware conversations
  • Fine-tuning: Custom datasets from special education contexts for domain-specific responses
  • Deployment: Ollama for local inference + Flask/FastAPI backend
